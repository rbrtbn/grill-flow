# Surface brief: grill page

Mode: Read, with Operate for the answer controls.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a decision you read like a gamebook, reasoning first, question at the end of each
section; it refuses the urgency-sorted card list.
OWN-WORLD: cool paper, one cobalt ink, Literata column, condensed numerals in the margin, "turn to"
links.
STORY: what we decide, why now, then one plain question whose options name their consequence.
SIGNATURE: section numbers out of order with "Turn to Qn" between them; the tree is the contents.
RAISES: from the cutting-bench challenger, state as a mark (filled, dashed, struck); from the
provenance challenger, certainty kept in words ("tested", "untested") rather than colour.
RISK: a gamebook reads as playful; kept to numbering and links, never ornament.
