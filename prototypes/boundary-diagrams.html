What the wiki owns

○LAB-914's accepted W1 plan puts reminders and closed-session leftovers in reminders.md. L5 then proposes a sent line so a recycled lead does not send a reminder twice. That was a reasonable open direction before this source comparison; changing it is your choice.

Current cos-rob already has the operating pattern: its role reads Scheduled entries in open-tasks.md, creates one-shot timers, replies with a stable --once key, then marks the wiki entry done. scripts/cos deduplicates queue ids under a lock. The listener advances its cursor before delivery to avoid duplicate posts after an unknown outcome; that can lose a post. Queued and posted are different.

I recommend that Squad's wiki retain lasting preferencespreference Non-authorizing guidance. It grants no action and loses to rules., corrections and evidenced lessons, plus references to unresolved work. An existing owner in the same environment keeps due times, execution and delivery status. Where no such owner exists, keep the unresolved request visible as pending work; this proposal does not create a CoS service or silently send a personal reminder through work Slack.

This changes the old W1 reminder boundary while preserving the knowledge repository, history and citations. It reduces duplicated state. It also means the wiki does not provide a new delivery guarantee. If you want operational reminders.md retained, we should design its owner and failure semantics explicitly before considering L5.

Either answer leaves lifecycle details open: trials/review dates, expiry, conflicts, citation checks, same-turn durability and each old visibility/leftovers idea will get its own later decision.
