# Grill-flow

Grill-flow carries source-owned questions, updates and owner work on shared reading pages.
The language keeps a request, evidence of delivery and a reported result distinct.

## Language

**Source**:
The grill that owns an item and its wording, even when another page displays it.
_Avoid_: Shared question number as an item's identity

**Owner step**:
An exact action Rob needs to perform at a named destination, with a reason and urgency.
_Avoid_: Question, grant

**Reported completion**:
The owning agent's statement that an owner step is complete, supported by a dated proof link.
_Avoid_: Independently verified completion

**Blocking condition**:
A named prerequisite whose answer or evidence can be met, unmet or unknown.
_Avoid_: Permission, guard, grant

**Evidence**:
A dated report and proof about the specific prerequisite being checked.
_Avoid_: Permission to proceed

**Provisional decision**:
A source-owned ruling offered with a deadline for requesting a veto.
_Avoid_: Granted authority

**Veto request**:
Rob's request that the owner reconsider or stop a specific provisional decision.
_Avoid_: Stopped action, grant

**Veto receipt**:
A durable account of a veto request, including the exact decision wording and its deadline.
_Avoid_: Grant receipt

**Delivery evidence**:
A report that a veto reached its destination terminal, separately from the owner's response.
_Avoid_: Read, acknowledged, stopped

**Owner acknowledgment**:
The owning agent's report that it received a veto request.
_Avoid_: Successful transport, confirmed outcome

**Owner outcome**:
The owning agent's report that it stopped or continued the action after a veto request.
_Avoid_: Verified runtime state
